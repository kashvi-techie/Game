# Controls

- `W` or Up Arrow: throttle
- `S` or Down Arrow: brake
- `A` / `D` or Left / Right Arrow: steer
- `Space`: nitro in a car; airbrake in the aircraft
- `Shift`: handbrake in a car
- `R`: recover the car or aircraft
- `H`: honk the car horn
- `V`: cycle the camera view
- `F`: switch between car and aircraft while exploring the society
- `G`: toggle aircraft landing gear
- Up / Down Arrow: aircraft pitch
- `Escape`: skip the intro, cancel the start countdown, pause a session, or resume it

The **AIRCRAFT** / **CAR** button in the top bar also switches vehicles. Switching to the aircraft works anywhere and places it where the car is. Switch back after landing, extending the gear, and stopping. The `F` key follows the same rules.

People display text reactions above their heads; no NPC voice audio is used. Audio can be muted or adjusted with the on-screen volume control. The Web Audio graph initializes only after a user gesture.
